Application information

Curious in 40 AutoTube

AutoTube is a personal desktop application operated by the Curious in 40 channel owner. It is not a public service and does not offer visitor accounts or a sign-in form on this website.

Its Google connection requests permission to view the authorized YouTube account and upload YouTube videos. Authorization credentials stay in a protected folder on the owner's computer. This public website contains only application information.

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Google consent screen. The channel is also available at @GetCuriousIn40.
